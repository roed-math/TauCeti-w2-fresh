# Lookahead: `relative-proj-closed-immersion` (SchemeAndStackFoundations, Layer 0, §0.3)

Target (README §0.3): if `A_1` is of finite type and `A` is generated in degree one, the graded
map `Sym_{O_S} A_1 → A` is surjective in every positive degree and induces a closed immersion
`relativeProj A → relativeProj (Sym_{O_S} A_1)` over `S` pulling `O(1)` back to `O(1)`
(Stacks 01N0, 01N1), so `relativeProj A → S` is projective.

Built on `main` at `333c9e49143d`. Status: **partial** (splits 1–5 proved; split 6 remains).
Resumed from tip `61d101d30848` (seventh round) and merged `main`; the merge was clean.

## What is proved and what remains

Proved (no `sorry` anywhere on the branch): for `A` a graded quasicoherent algebra on `X`
generated in degree one on affine charts, `Sym A₁ ⟶ A` is surjective in every positive degree on
affine sections and an epimorphism of module sheaves in every positive degree, and induces a
closed immersion `Proj_X(A) ⟶ Proj_X(Sym A₁)` over `X` (splits 1–3); the chart computation of the
twisting-sheaf maps (split 4); and, for every morphism of graded algebras `φ : B ⟶ A` inducing
`f : Proj_X(A) ⟶ Proj_X(B)`, the glued morphism `f^* 𝒪_B(n) ⟶ 𝒪_A(n)` (split 5).

Remains (split 6):

- `isIso_relativeProjTwistingSheafMap`: for `φ = gradedSymmetricAlgebraLift` and `n = 1` (with
  `A` generated in degree one) the morphism of split 5 is an isomorphism. Plan: check it after
  restricting to the charts `ι_U` of `Proj_X(A)` (`SheafOfModules` isomorphisms are local);
  `restrictRelativeProjTwistingSheafIso` identifies `𝒪_A(1)|_U` with `Proj.twistingSheaf _ 1`, and
  `(D.restrictGluedIso _ U).hom ∘ (lift …)|_U` is the adjoint of
  `relativeProjTwistingSheafMapComponent U` (from `lift_π` and `restrictGluedHom`'s definition as
  `homEquiv.symm (π U)`). On the chart this is the adjoint of `Proj.twistingSheafMap g 1`, an
  isomorphism on the cover by `D₊(g f)`, `f ∈ (Sym A₁)(U)₁` (cover by `Proj.iSup_basicOpen_eq_top'`
  as in `isInvertible_relativeProjTwistingSheaf`), because `𝒪(1)|D₊(f)` is free on `f` and
  `𝒪(1)|D₊(g f)` free on `g f`, and the map sends one generator to the other (split 4,
  `twistingSheafMap_app_chartSectionsLinearEquiv`).
- The exported `relativeProj_closedImmersion` bundling the closed immersion over `S`, the `O(1)`
  isomorphism, and "so `relativeProj A → S` is projective" with `A_1` of finite type: Tau Ceti has
  no projective-morphism predicate on `main`, so that conclusion is stated as the closed immersion
  into `Proj_X(Sym A₁)` over `S` with `A₁` of finite type, plus the `O(1)` pullback.
