# Lookahead: `gamma-zero-naive` (ModularCurves, Layer 3N)

Built on `main` at `52f9d081237dadaf6d4eae13629281f7faa37d54`. Status: **partial**.

The target is Layer 3N of `ModularCurves/README.md`, *Naive level structures*. The naive `[Γ(N)]`
and `[Γ₁(N)]` problems, their relative representability and their étaleness were already on
`main` (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/NaiveLevel.lean`). The remaining clause is the
naive `[Γ₀(N)]` problem: "a finite locally free subgroup scheme `C ⊆ E[N]`, of order `N`,
étale-locally generated by a single section", relatively representable and étale over
`Ell/ℤ[1/N]`, with carrier "the finite étale quotient of the full-basis carrier by the Borel,
using the quotient theorem of Layer 0C", identified "with the cyclic open-and-closed locus in the
subgroup parameter space of Layer 0G". `Suggested.lean` pins no declaration for Layer 3N, so the
names follow the neighbouring code on `main` (`gammaNaive`, `gammaOneNaive`, `gammaZero`).
