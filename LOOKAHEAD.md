# Lookahead: `relative-proj-closed-immersion` (SchemeAndStackFoundations, Layer 0, §0.3)

Target (README §0.3): if `A_1` is of finite type and `A` is generated in degree one, the graded
map `Sym_{O_S} A_1 → A` is surjective in every positive degree and induces a closed immersion
`relativeProj A → relativeProj (Sym_{O_S} A_1)` over `S` pulling `O(1)` back to `O(1)`
(Stacks 01N0, 01N1), so `relativeProj A → S` is projective.

## What is proved and what remains

Proved (no `sorry` outside the stub file): for `A` a graded quasicoherent algebra on `X` generated
in degree one on affine charts, `Sym A₁ ⟶ A` is surjective in every positive degree on affine
sections and an epimorphism of module sheaves in every positive degree, and induces a closed
immersion `Proj_X(A) ⟶ Proj_X(Sym A₁)` over `X` (splits 1–3); the chart computation of the
twisting-sheaf maps (split 4); and, against the stubs, the chart components of the morphism of
twisting sheaves (split 5, partial).

Remains:

  `chart V ≫ f_*.map ((A.relativeProjTwistingSheafGlueData n).transition i) = chart U` for
  `i : U ⟶ V`. Proof plan, all pieces present: `Scheme.Modules.hom_ext` and

  `transition_app_apply`; generalize `(π V).app W s`; then `Proj.twistingSheaf.ext` and
  `comap_apply`/`res_apply` reduce to `localizationsMap_comp_comm` with
  `gradedSectionsGradedHom_comp_restrictGradedSectionsGradedHom φ _`. **Obstacle met this round:**
  the glued modules live on `colimit X.relativeProjGluingData.functor` while `relativeProjMap`
  has type `A.relativeProj ⟶ B.relativeProj` (`relativeProj` is a non-reducible `def`), so
  `simp only [Scheme.Modules.Hom.comp_app, …]` does not fire on the mixed composites, and a
  `rfl` proof of the sections formula for `chart U` hits the heartbeat limit. Remedy for the
  next round: state a `rfl` sections lemma for each pushforward-isomorphism step separately, or

  does) and keep every scheme in `relativeProj` form.
