# Lookahead: `relative-proj-closed-immersion` (SchemeAndStackFoundations, Layer 0, §0.3)

Target (README §0.3): if `A_1` is of finite type and `A` is generated in degree one, the graded
map `Sym_{O_S} A_1 → A` is surjective in every positive degree and induces a closed immersion
`relativeProj A → relativeProj (Sym_{O_S} A_1)` over `S` pulling `O(1)` back to `O(1)`
(Stacks 01N0, 01N1), so `relativeProj A → S` is projective.

Built on `main` at `333c9e49143d`. Status: **partial** (splits 1–6 proved; split 7 remains).
Resumed from tip `69396765fe45` (eighth round); `main` had not moved, so there was nothing to merge.
This round proved split 6 (the glued morphism `f^* 𝒪(n) ⟶ 𝒪(n)` is an isomorphism under a covering
hypothesis) and the covering hypothesis for `Sym A₁ ⟶ A` (in split 3).

## What is proved and what remains

Proved (no `sorry` anywhere on the branch): for `A` a graded quasicoherent algebra on `X`
generated in degree one on affine charts, `Sym A₁ ⟶ A` is surjective in every positive degree on
affine sections and an epimorphism of module sheaves in every positive degree, and induces a
closed immersion `Proj_X(A) ⟶ Proj_X(Sym A₁)` over `X` (splits 1–3); the chart computation of the
twisting-sheaf maps (split 4); for every morphism of graded algebras `φ : B ⟶ A` inducing
`f : Proj_X(A) ⟶ Proj_X(B)`, the glued morphism `f^* 𝒪_B(n) ⟶ 𝒪_A(n)` (split 5), and that it is
an isomorphism whenever `𝒪_B(n)` is invertible and the `D₊(φ b)`, `b` of degree one, cover the
charts of `Proj_X(A)` (split 6); for `φ = Sym A₁ ⟶ A` with `A` generated in degree one, that
covering holds (`exists_mem_basicOpen_gradedSymmetricAlgebraLift`, split 3).

Remains (split 7), in the order to do them:

- **`Sym F` is generated in degree one on affine sections**, for a quasicoherent `F` (here
  `F = A₁`): over an affine open `U`, `Algebra.adjoin Γ(X, U) (Γ(Sym⁰ F, U) ∪ Γ(Sym¹ F, U)) = ⊤`
  in `⨁ n, Γ(Symⁿ F, U)`. With `adjoin_one_eq_top_of_adjoin_zero_union_one_eq_top` and
  `CommMon.isInvertible_relativeProjTwistingSheaf` this makes `𝒪(n)` invertible on
  `Proj_X(Sym A₁)`, the remaining hypothesis of `isIso_relativeProjTwistingSheafMap`. Main has the
  sheaf-level fact (`epi_mul_gradedSymmetricAlgebra`: `Symⁱ F ⊗ Symʲ F ⟶ Symⁱ⁺ʲ F` is epi) and
  surjectivity on affine sections of epimorphisms of quasicoherent sheaves
  (`Scheme.Modules.Hom.app_surjective_of_epi_of_isAffineOpen`), and the product of the graded ring
  of sections is `μ` followed by the multiplication (`CommMon.sections_gMul_def`). What is missing
  is that `μ : Γ(M, U) ⊗ Γ(N, U) ⟶ Γ(M ⊗ N, U)` (the tensor map of `Scheme.Modules.sectionsFunctor`)
  is surjective for quasicoherent `M`, `N` and affine `U`; then induct on the degree with
  `Sym¹ ⊗ Symᵈ ⟶ Symᵈ⁺¹`. (Alternatively go through `tildeSymmetricPowerIso` on `Spec`.) This
  was not available on `main` and did not fit in this round.
- The exported `relativeProj_closedImmersion` bundling the closed immersion over `S`
  (`isClosedImmersion_relativeProjMap_gradedSymmetricAlgebraLift`), the `O(1)` isomorphism
  (`isIso_relativeProjTwistingSheafMap` at `n = 1`, with the covering from
  `exists_mem_basicOpen_gradedSymmetricAlgebraLift U.toOpens U.2` and the invertibility above), and
  "so `relativeProj A → S` is projective" with `A_1` of finite type: Tau Ceti has no
  projective-morphism predicate on `main`, so that conclusion is stated as the closed immersion
  into `Proj_X(Sym A₁)` over `S` with `A₁` of finite type, plus the `O(1)` pullback. The branch
  states *generated in degree one* as `Algebra.adjoin Γ(X, U) (A₀ ∪ A₁) = ⊤` on affine opens; the
  README's form `Algebra.adjoin A₀ A₁ = ⊤` is equivalent (one direction is
  `adjoin_one_eq_top_of_adjoin_zero_union_one_eq_top`), and the exported theorem should take the
  README's form, adding the easy converse.
